# CoTailr FAQ

## About CoTailr

**What is CoTailr?**
An AI resume and cover-letter tailoring app at [cotailr.com](https://cotailr.com). Paste a job description or link
and get a resume, cover letter and combined application pack tailored to that job, built only from your own
profile and rendered in ATS-safe layouts. It also scores job fit, tracks applications and drafts screening-question
answers. More in [What is CoTailr?](what-is-cotailr.md)

**Who makes it?**
NexNimbus LLP, Bangalore, India. Contact: connect@nexnimbus.com.

**Is there a free tier?**
Yes. New accounts get a 7-day Pro trial with 25 credits, no card required. After that, Free gives 1.5 credits a day
(reset at midnight IST) with the ATS Classic template and resume PDFs. Plus ($9.99 / ₹499 a month) and Pro
($19.99 / ₹999 a month) add Tech Style in every size, cover letters and full packs, Job Fit and an unlimited
tracker. See [pricing](https://cotailr.com/pricing/).

## Accuracy and trust

**Will it invent experience I don't have?**
Tailoring draws only on your profile. You control each line with a mode: **Locked** lines print exactly as
written, **Preferred** lines may be reworded but not replaced, **Flexible** lines are swapped in only when they fit
the job better, and **CoTailr AI** slots are filled from your master resume and the job. After each pass, an
invent-check compares numbers and dates in the output against your profile, and on Plus and Pro it flags
anything that doesn't trace back so you can review it.

**Will my resume pass ATS scanning?**
Every template renders flow-based HTML to PDF through a real browser engine, so text extracts in reading order.
ATS Classic is a single-column, parser-first family for Workday, Greenhouse and LinkedIn Easy Apply. No tool can
guarantee a recruiter outcome, and CoTailr doesn't claim to.

## Using CoTailr

**I don't have a polished resume. Can I still start?**
Yes. Paste or upload whatever you have (PDF, DOCX, TXT or Markdown) and *Organise with CoTailr AI* structures it.

**Which job sites can it read?**
Workday, Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, SmartRecruiters, Workable, Recruitee, Personio, BambooHR, LinkedIn, Teamtailor,
Rippling and many more, plus a generic reader for other career pages. If a site blocks automated reading, paste
the text.

**What sizes are there?**
Snapshot (1 page), Professional (2), Portfolio (3–4) and Dossier (5+), or **Auto**, which picks the size from how
much of the job your profile covers. Tech Style ships all four sizes. ATS Classic, Minimal and Coral ship
Professional.

**Can I edit after generating?**
Yes. Edit the generated resume or cover letter in place, and saving re-renders the PDF without another credit.

**Can I control the writing voice?**
Yes. AI Tone sets seniority, style, authority, language (British, American or neutral international) and
personality.

**Does it help with application forms?**
Answers drafts replies to screening questions from your profile and Brief. It finds questions on Greenhouse, Ashby
and Lever postings or takes pasted ones, marks facts it doesn't have as "Not on file", and never submits anything
for you.

## AI assistants (connector and skill)

**Can I use CoTailr from Claude or Cursor?**
Yes. Create an access key in [Settings → Connected apps](https://cotailr.com/settings#connected-apps) and add the
CoTailr connector to Claude, Cursor, VS Code, Claude Code or any MCP app. Setup is in the
[README](../README.md#connect-your-app).

**Do I need the skill?**
No. The connector already tells the assistant how to use CoTailr and offers one-click workflows. The skill makes
Claude more consistent: it asks before spending credits, checks your Brief for salary questions, avoids duplicate
tracker entries and flags stale applications.

**Does using it through Claude cost extra?**
No. It uses your normal CoTailr credits, with a cap of 15 credits a day for connected apps. Reading, fetching jobs
and editing are free.

**Can the assistant mess up my profile?**
Every change made through the connector is marked in CoTailr with one-click Undo, and contact-detail changes need
your confirmation. *Generate only* keys can't edit anything.

**Is my key safe?**
Keys are stored hashed and can be revoked instantly. Never paste your key into a chat. Put it only in the app's
connector settings.

## Privacy

**Is my data private?**
CoTailr doesn't sell your personal information. Each account's data is isolated, generated files expire from
CoTailr's servers by default, and form screenshots are never saved. Full details in the
[Privacy Policy](https://cotailr.com/privacy/).
